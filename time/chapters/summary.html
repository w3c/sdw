Summary of Classes and Properties in the Time Ontology

  • Items in italics were added in the 2017 revision of OWL-Time and are not yet widely used. These may be considered features at risk.

    • Classes (organized to show subclass relations)

    • time:TemporalEntity
      • time:Instant
      • time:Interval
        • time:ProperInterval
          • time:DateTimeInterval
    • time:TemporalPosition
      • time:TimePosition
      • time:GeneralDateTimeDescription
        • time:DateTimeDescription
          • time:MonthOfYear (class)
    • time:TemporalDuration
      • time:Duration
      • time:GeneralDurationDescription
        • time:DurationDescription
      • time:TemporalUnit
    • time:TRS
    • time:TimeZone (class)
    • time:DayOfWeek (class)

    Datatypes

    • time:generalDay
    • time:generalMonth
    • time:generalYear

    Properties (organized by domain value)

    Domain time:TemporalEntity

    • time:hasBeginning rdfs:range time:Instant .
    • time:hasEnd rdfs:range time:Instant .
    • time:hasTemporalDuration rdfs:range time:TemporalDuration .
    • time:hasDuration rdfs:range time:Duration .
    • time:hasDurationDescription rdfs:range time:GeneralDurationDescription .
    • time:hasXSDDuration rdfs:range xsd:duration .
    • time:before rdfs:range time:TemporalEntity .
    • time:after rdfs:range time:TemporalEntity .

    Domain time:Instant

    • time:inTemporalPosition rdfs:range time:TemporalPosition .
    • time:inTimePosition rdfs:range time:TimePosition .
    • time:inDateTime rdfs:range time:GeneralDateTimeDescription .
    • time:inXSDDate rdfs:range xsd:date .
    • time:inXSDDateTime (deprecated) rdfs:range xsd:dateTime .
    • time:inXSDDateTimeStamp rdfs:range xsd:dateTimeStamp .
    • time:inXSDgYearMonth rdfs:range xsd:gYearMonth .
    • time:inXSDgYear rdfs:range xsd:gYear .

    Domain time:Interval

    • time:inside rdfs:range time:Instant .

    Domain time:ProperInterval

    • time:intervalEquals rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalDisjoint rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalAfter rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalBefore rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalMeets rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalMetBy rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalOverlaps rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalOverlappedBy rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalStarts rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalStartedBy rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalFinishes rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalFinishedBy rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalContains rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalDuring rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .
    • time:intervalIn rdfs:range time:ProperInterval .

    Domain time:DateTimeInterval

    • time:hasDateTimeDescription rdfs:range time:GeneralDateTimeDescription .
    • time:xsdDateTime (deprecated) rdfs:range xsd:dateTime .

    Domain time:TemporalPosition or time:GeneralDurationDescription

    • time:hasTRS rdfs:range time:TRS .

    Domain time:TimePosition

    • time:numericPosition rdfs:range xsd:decimal .
    • time:nominalPosition rdfs:range xsd:string .

    Domain time:GeneralDateTimeDescription

    • time:timeZone rdfs:range time:TimeZone (class) .

    • time:month

    • time:minute rdfs:range xsd:nonNegativeInteger .
    • time:second rdfs:range xsd:decimal .
    • time:week rdfs:range xsd:nonNegativeInteger .
    • time:dayOfYear rdfs:range xsd:nonNegativeInteger .
    • time:dayOfWeek rdfs:range time:DayOfWeek (class) .
    • time:monthOfYear rdfs:range time:MonthOfYear (class) .

    Domain time:GeneralDateTimeDescription or time:Duration

    • time:unitType rdfs:range time:TemporalUnit .

    Domain time:Duration

    • time:numericDuration rdfs:range xsd:decimal .

    Domain time:GeneralDurationDescription

    • time:years rdfs:range xsd:decimal .

    • time:weeks rdfs:range xsd:decimal .
    • time:days rdfs:range xsd:decimal .
    • time:hours rdfs:range xsd:decimal .
    • time:minutes rdfs:range xsd:decimal .
    • time:seconds rdfs:range xsd:decimal .
